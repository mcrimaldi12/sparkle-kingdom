# Sparkle Kingdom 👑

A princess, kitten, puppy and unicorn learning app for kids in 1st to 3rd grade. One HTML file, works offline, no accounts, nothing leaves the device.

**Reading Tower:** Magic Words (sight-word flash cards), Kitten Calls (hear a word, tap it), Castle Sentences (build a sentence), Fairy Fill-In (missing word), Story Scrolls (read a short story and answer), Jewel Match (memory).

**Math Garden:** Cupcake Adding, Puppy Take-Away, Bunny Count / Royal Coins / Unicorn Times (by grade), Princess Challenge, Pet Problems (word problems), Bigger or Smaller.

**Royal Pet Palace:** every right answer earns a star; stars unlock 20 animal friends.

**Grown-ups:** press and hold the ⚙️ for 2 seconds to pick 1st, 2nd or 3rd grade, turn the voice on or off, set her name, and see progress.
